Description: Acacia, also called akashmoni, is a bushy kind of tree that takes a lot of time to mature. It is known for its huge thorns. Though it is not as durable as teak wood, furniture made from solid acacia wood can still last for many long years, and hence serves as a cheaper alternative to teak wood.

Usage/Application: Furniture, Veneer, Home Construction, Wooden flooring

Colors: Light brown and dark red

Wood Type: Hard Wood

Botanical Name: Azadiracta Indica
