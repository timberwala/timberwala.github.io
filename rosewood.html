Description: A rosewood tree takes 100 to 200 years to grow; each ring on the log represents one year of its life. The older the tree, the closer the grains. Rosewood is a hardwood and Indian rosewood belongs to the Dalbergia botanical family. It has colour variations ranging from purple, blue and brown to red, and is widely used in the guitar industry for its appearance and acoustical properties.

Usage/Application: Acoustic and Electrical Guitars, Furniture, Veneer, Handicraft, Home Construction
